The model compares the volatility the pool's fees are pricing in (√(8·feeAPR)) against the asset's realized and options-implied volatility. When fees price in more volatility than actually occurs, LPs are being overpaid.

Model: LVR cost = σ²⁄8 (Milionis–Moallemi–Roughgarden–Zhang). Breakeven σ = fee-implied σ = √(8·feeAPR) (optimistic, full-range; swap fees only).
